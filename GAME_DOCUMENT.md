# Magical Byte Academy — The Enchanted Help Desk

**Edition:** Cinematic Fantasy V2 · **Course:** 273411, Communicative English for Computer and Information Technology

**Target learners:** Beginner-to-intermediate university Computer Science and IT students.

**Learning objectives:** Greet visitors professionally; ask diagnostic questions; explain safe troubleshooting steps and their purpose; verify outcomes; close politely. Practice IT vocabulary across connectivity, software, hardware, teaching platforms, spreadsheets, backups, networking, security, and privacy.

**Role and story:** You are the Magical IT Advisor in the Magical Technology Support Chamber. Luna, Mix, Direx, Celeste, Noah, Professor Ivy, Aurora, Felix, Mochi, and Serena visit with technical problems. Twelve story cases across four chapters lead to an ongoing support practice mode. Returning visitors remember previous help through relationship-dependent dialogue.

**How to play:** Start or Continue. Read a visitor’s dialogue and the response goal. Type a clear English sentence, or use supported microphone recognition and review, edit, and confirm the transcript before submitting. Read the educational explanation; retry or use Hint and guided phrases. Complete five turns per case. Use the journal for progress, vocabulary, achievements, and practice replay.

**Core loop:** Visitor arrives → English input → rule-based feedback → diagnosis and advice → verified outcome → professional closing → rewards → next visitor. Endless Support cycles 30 authored templates with shuffled order, varied contexts, technical vocabulary, and difficulty. Advanced turns require a purpose explanation. The mode reuses authored content rather than claiming AI-generated novelty.

**Progress:** Story Magic Stars, XP, satisfaction, friendships, chapters, and achievements. Endless statistics and friendships are separate; story replay never duplicates main rewards. LocalStorage saves the active turn and draft. V1 saves migrate without resetting progress.

**Technology and actual AI use:** HTML5, CSS3, vanilla JavaScript, WebP images, and LocalStorage. OpenAI Codex assisted with code, dialogue, and tests. OpenAI image generation produced an original fantasy chamber and ten portraits. Visuals are rendered 3D-style illustrations animated with CSS; there is no real-time 3D engine. Runtime evaluation and case selection use authored rules, not a language model. No required backend, paid API, or API key is used.

**Responsible evaluation:** Rules check selected intent synonyms, technical terms, politeness, and length; they cannot fully judge grammar or meaning. Feedback explains limits and offers guided phrases. Scores are game mechanics, not validated proficiency scores. Browser speech recognition may process audio remotely; the game does not store audio or score pronunciation. Automated tests cover story and Endless progression, migration, replay protection, and simulated speech states. Real microphones and learning effectiveness require human review. No human playtesting results or team contributions are invented.

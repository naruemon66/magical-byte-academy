# Magical Byte Academy — The Enchanted Help Desk

**Edition:** Cinematic Fantasy V2 · **Course:** 273411, Communicative English for Computer and Information Technology

**Target learners:** Beginner-to-intermediate university Computer Science and IT students.

**Learning objectives:** Greet visitors professionally; ask diagnostic questions; explain safe troubleshooting steps and their purpose; verify outcomes; close politely. Practice IT vocabulary across connectivity, software, hardware, teaching platforms, spreadsheets, backups, networking, security, and privacy.

**Role and story:** You are the Magical IT Advisor in the Magical Technology Support Chamber. Luna, Mix, Direx, Celeste, Noah, Professor Ivy, Aurora, Felix, Mochi, and Serena visit with technical problems. Twelve story cases across four chapters lead to an ongoing support practice mode. Returning visitors remember previous help through relationship-dependent dialogue.

**How to play:** Start or Continue, enter an advisor nickname, and read a visitor’s dialogue and the response goal. Choose among four communication responses, type a clear English sentence, or use supported microphone recognition and review, edit, and confirm the transcript before submitting. Read the educational explanation; retry or use Hint and guided phrases. Complete five turns per case. Use the journal for progress, vocabulary, achievements, and practice replay.

**Core loop:** Visitor arrives → English input → rule-based feedback → diagnosis and advice → verified outcome → professional closing → rewards → next visitor. Endless Support cycles 35 authored templates with shuffled order, varied contexts, technical vocabulary, and difficulty. Advanced turns require a purpose explanation. The mode reuses authored content rather than claiming AI-generated novelty.

**Unit 4:** Practice polite requests, offers, suggestions, shared decisions, and Disagree + Suggest through Sprint Planning, Code Review, Asking for Help, Blockers, and teammate advice. Direct, Polite, and Professional feedback distinguishes technical correctness from appropriate communication. Type, Choose, and microphone transcripts use the same evaluator. Team practice is available through the journal without duplicating main rewards.

**Progress:** A quest-order panel and advisor level/score display guide progression. Completed quests have 1–5 stars and best-score replay, without duplicate XP or main rewards. Story Magic Stars, XP, satisfaction, friendships, chapters, and achievements. Endless statistics and friendships are separate; story replay never duplicates main rewards. LocalStorage saves the active turn and draft. V1 saves migrate without resetting progress.

**Technology and actual AI use:** HTML5, CSS3, vanilla JavaScript, WebP images, original instrumental Web Audio music with mute/volume and microphone pause, and LocalStorage. OpenAI Codex assisted with code, dialogue, and tests. OpenAI image generation produced an original fantasy chamber and ten portraits. Visuals are rendered 3D-style illustrations animated with CSS; there is no real-time 3D engine. Runtime evaluation and case selection use authored rules, not a language model. No required backend, paid API, or API key is used.

**Responsible evaluation:** Rules check selected intent synonyms, technical terms, politeness, and length; they cannot fully judge grammar or meaning. Interactive feedback cards explain limits, award partial credit to minor MCQ errors, and offer three model replies. Unsafe advice remains blocked. Scores are game mechanics, not validated proficiency scores. Browser speech recognition may process audio remotely; the game does not store audio or score pronunciation. Automated tests cover story and Endless progression, migration, replay protection, and simulated speech states. Real microphones and learning effectiveness require human review. No human playtesting results or team contributions are invented.
